How it works

One link. Four steps. Zero timeline scrubbing.

Every clip goes through the same pipeline you would do by hand, minus the two hours in an editor.

STEP 1 · IMPORT

Paste a link or drop a file

YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, X, Vimeo or a local MP4. Up to 4K source, or several links at once to queue them.

STEP 2 · TRANSCRIBE

Every word, timestamped

Whisper speech recognition with word-level timing and proper punctuation, in 90+ languages.

STEP 3 · FIND MOMENTS

Scores the whole video

Ranks every possible clip by hook, payoff, energy and audience replays, then cuts on real sentence boundaries.

STEP 4 · CREATE CLIPS

Reframed, captioned, done

9:16 at 1080×1920 with animated captions, loudness normalized and ready to upload.

Moment detection

Smart reframe

Landscape in, perfectly framed vertical out.

Face tracking follows whoever is on screen with a smooth virtual camera that only moves when it has to. Two people far apart get a split screen. Slides and b-roll get the full frame over a blurred background, so nothing is cut off.

The crop window glides to the active speaker and holds still between moves, so the shot never jitters.

The details

Built for people who clip every day.

GPU acceleration

One click installs NVIDIA support. The most accurate speech model transcribes a 14-minute talk in about a minute on an RTX 3050.

Edit, then re-render

Fix any caption word, swap styles, nudge the start and end, or change the reframe mode. Re-renders take seconds.

Queue and walk away

Paste several links or press Clip it on a few Discover picks. They run one after another, with progress on your taskbar.

Platform-ready audio

Every clip is mastered to −14 LUFS, the loudness TikTok, Shorts and Reels play at.

Export your way

Save all clips to a folder, copy titles and hashtags, export captions as .srt, or jump to the original at the clip's timestamp.

Private by design

Video, audio and transcription stay on your PC. An optional API key is encrypted with Windows DPAPI.

FAQ

Questions.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

No. Downloading, transcription, reframing and rendering all happen on your computer. The only optional exception is Claude AI mode, which sends the transcript text to Anthropic to pick moments.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. Everything runs on the CPU. With an NVIDIA GPU, transcription is several times faster and encoding uses NVENC.

How accurate are the captions?

You choose between four accuracy levels, up to Whisper large-v3-turbo. Words are individually timed, punctuated and capitalized, and any word can be corrected before re-rendering.

How long does a video take?

A 14-minute talk became four finished clips in about a minute and a half on a mid-range PC, including the download. Longer videos scale with their length.

Can I clip any video I find?

Only clip videos you have the rights to use, or that the creator allows. Many podcasts and streamers run official clipping programs, and those are a good place to start.
